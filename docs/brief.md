**Company History**

DOLF VISUALS is a creative photography, videography, and visual production brand officially established on October 11, 2025, in Tanzania.

The brand was born following the loss of the entire database belonging to its predecessor, KHAMY_PHOTOGRAPHY, at the end of September 2025. This setback marked the beginning of a new chapter, creating an opportunity to rebuild, establish a stronger brand identity, and pursue greater ambitions in

<img src="media/image6.png" style="width:18.375in;height:25.97222in" />

the visual production industry.

Since its establishment, DOLF VISUALS has been committed to delivering professional photography, videography, and creative visual content, with a strong focus on quality, creativity, professionalism, and client satisfaction.

On October 11, 2026, DOLF VISUALS celebrated its first anniversary, marking one year of growth, resilience, creativity, and commitment to building a brand capable of competing at international production standards.
